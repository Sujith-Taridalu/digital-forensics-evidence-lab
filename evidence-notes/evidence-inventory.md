# Evidence Inventory

## Primary Evidence

- forensic disk image of the suspect system
- validated image hash used to support integrity

## Key Artifact Sources

- `Windows Prefetch`
- `AppData/Roaming/Discord/Cache`
- browser history and SQLite-backed history data
- `Documents`, `Downloads`, and `Desktop` folders
- suspicious archives and image files

## Artifacts of Interest

- Discord-related communication traces
- files associated with steganography or concealment
- encrypted-volume tooling indicators
- downloaded archives with operational relevance
- browsing patterns related to encryption, travel, and concealment
