# Executive Summary

This case study documents the forensic review of a seized disk image in a criminal investigation scenario. The analysis focused on uncovering communication evidence, signs of concealment, suspicious files, and user activity that could indicate illegal coordination.

The investigation revealed a pattern of communication artifacts, file transfers, and tool usage consistent with attempts to coordinate activity and obscure evidence. Application traces, local cache data, browser history, and on-disk files reinforced one another and allowed the findings to be presented as a coherent investigative narrative.

Limitations remained where encrypted or hidden content could not be fully recovered without additional information. Even with those constraints, the image contained enough artifact correlation to justify deeper follow-on investigation.
